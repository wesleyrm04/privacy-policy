## 1. Overview
JCM AutoPost Manager ("we", "our", or "us") is a desktop productivity application built to help manage and automate content publishing to authorized Facebook Pages for business marketing and promotional workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

## 2. Information Collection and Local Storage
- **Local Database:** JCM AutoPost Manager operates primarily as a local desktop application. All content libraries, schedules, post queues, and application settings are stored locally on your device within a secure SQLite database (`data/jcm_autopost.db`).
- **Credentials and Tokens:** Page Access Tokens required to authenticate and publish content to your authorized Facebook Pages are stored securely using local system credential management. We do not transmit or store your access tokens on any external remote servers.

## 3. How We Use Information
The data processed by the application is used strictly for:
- Executing automated publishing schedules and queue rotations for your authorized Facebook Pages via the Meta Graph API.
- Managing local print shop job templates, captions, and media assets.

## 4. Third-Party Services (Meta / Facebook)
Our application interacts with the official Meta Graph API to publish posts, photos, videos, and reels to Facebook Pages. Your interactions with Facebook are governed by Meta’s Terms of Service and Data Policy. The application only requests the specific permissions required to publish content to the pages you explicitly manage and connect.

## 5. Data Security
Because your database, logs, and backups reside entirely on your local machine, you retain full ownership and control over your data. Local automated backups and SQLite integrity measures are built into the app to prevent data corruption.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in application features or compliance standards. Any updates will be posted directly within our repository.
